Where the time goes today

None of this is hard work. It is work that arrives at the wrong time.

  • Portal leads go cold overnight

    Enquiries from listing portals arrive in the evening and at weekends. By the time someone replies the next morning, the buyer has often messaged other agents.

  • Agents qualify by hand

    Every new lead means the same questions about budget, timing, area and financing, typed out between viewings.

  • Viewings booked by back-and-forth

    Finding a slot takes several messages, and the no-shows are the ones nobody reminded.

  • The CRM is a week behind

    Notes live in phones and inboxes, so the Monday pipeline meeting runs on memory.

What we set up

Each one is a separate piece. Start with the first, add the others when it has earned its keep.

  • Instant lead reply

    What starts it
    A new enquiry from a listing portal, your website form, WhatsApp or a missed call.
    What it does
    Replies in about a minute by text, email or WhatsApp, in your team's tone, with the details of the listing they asked about.
    What a person still does
    Nothing, unless the buyer asks for a person. Then it hands over with the conversation attached.

  • Lead qualification

    What starts it
    The buyer or seller replies.
    What it does
    Asks the questions your ISA would ask: budget, timing, area, financing, whether they have a home to sell. Writes a short summary and a priority.
    What a person still does
    Agents read the summary and decide who to call first.

  • Viewing booking and reminders

    What starts it
    A qualified lead wants to see a property.
    What it does
    Offers free slots from the agent's calendar, books the viewing, and sends a reminder the day before and an hour before.
    What a person still does
    The agent shows the home.

  • CRM logging and lead routing

    What starts it
    Any new lead or booked viewing.
    What it does
    Creates or updates the contact, attaches the conversation, and assigns the lead by area, price band or round-robin.
    What a person still does
    The team lead sets the routing rules and can change them at any time.

  • Long-term follow-up

    What starts it
    A lead says not yet, or goes quiet.
    What it does
    Checks in on a schedule you approve, with new listings that match what they asked for.
    What a person still does
    An agent picks up as soon as the lead replies.

What it will and will not do

  • It says it is an assistant

    Messages make clear the buyer is talking to an automated assistant for your team, and offer a person at any point.

  • It stays inside the listing

    The assistant answers from your listing data and the rules you set. It does not negotiate, quote a price that is not on the listing, or give legal or mortgage advice.

  • Fair housing and local rules

    Qualifying questions are written with you and limited to budget, timing and property needs. You review the script against the fair housing and advertising rules that apply where you work before it goes live.

  • Consent for texts and calls

    Rules on texting and calling leads differ by country and state. We build in opt-out handling and quiet hours, and you confirm the consent wording for your market.

  • You own the accounts

    The CRM, phone number, WhatsApp account and workflow accounts are in your name. If we stop working together, everything keeps running.

Questions we get asked

Will buyers know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. The first message says it is the team's assistant, and anyone can ask for a person. Late at night, a clear answer and a time slot is usually what the enquiry was for.

What happens when a lead asks something it cannot answer?

It says so, takes the question, and passes the conversation to the right agent with a summary. It does not guess at prices, offers or legal points.

We already have an ISA. Does this replace them?

No. It covers the hours and the volume a person cannot, and does the first reply and the basic questions. Your ISA spends their time on the calls that need a person.

Does it work with our CRM and portals?

If your CRM has an API, a Zapier or Make connection, or can receive email, almost certainly. Portal leads usually arrive by email or a feed, which is enough. We check your exact setup in the audit before quoting.

How long before it is live?

A first pilot, usually instant reply and qualification for one lead source, takes two to four weeks including a test period on real enquiries.

A typical first project

Start with the one lead source that loses the most enquiries. Prove it on real leads, then decide whether to add the rest.
