Why work with a one-person studio

Four things are different from hiring a larger agency. Here is how each one works in practice.

One person does the work

You talk to the person who builds it. The questions you ask on a call are answered by someone who has your workflow open on the other screen.

  • Your audit call, build and support are all with Aditya
  • One shared channel, on email, WhatsApp or Slack, whichever you already use
  • Honest limit: one person means a small number of clients at a time, and a start date rather than a start tomorrow

Fast delivery

The pilot is one narrow workflow, live in 2 to 4 weeks. It runs on your real enquiries, calls or invoices, not a demo.

  • Week one: access, sample data and a written map of the process
  • Then a working version you can test, with changes made as you use it
  • The 2 to 4 weeks depends on getting access to your tools in the first few days

You own all accounts

Everything is built inside your own n8n, Make or Zapier workspace, your own CRM and your own API accounts. I am invited in as a user, and you can remove me.

  • Subscriptions and API usage are billed to you directly, at cost, with no markup
  • No shared passwords: access is by invitation, with its own login
  • Your customer data stays in the tools you already chose

No lock-in

The retainer is month to month. If you stop, the automations keep running in your accounts and you keep everything needed to run or change them.

  • A full handover document: what each workflow does, where it lives and how to fix the common faults
  • Workflows are exportable, so another builder or your own staff can take over
  • Cancel the retainer with a month's notice, in one email

What we say no to

It is cheaper for both of us to find this out now.

Work that happens a few times a month
If a task takes an hour a week, the setup fee will take years to earn back. I will tell you that on the audit call.
Decisions that need a licensed professional
An automation can collect details, book the appointment and send reminders. It should not diagnose a patient, give legal or tax advice, or approve a payment on its own.
Cold outreach at volume
No scraped lists, no mass unsolicited WhatsApp or email. It gets accounts banned and it is not the kind of work I want my name on.
Projects that need a team on call around the clock
One person cannot promise a 15-minute response at 3 am. If your process needs that, you need a larger provider, and I will say so.
Building inside accounts you do not own
If the plan only works when everything sits in my accounts, I will not build it. Ownership by the client is the whole arrangement.
A process nobody can describe yet
If the steps change every time, automating it locks in the mess. We write the process down first, and sometimes that alone fixes it.
