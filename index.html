Questions

Questions owners ask first

Something else on your mind? Bring it to the audit call. It is free and there is no pitch at the end.

Book a free AI audit
What happens in the free AI audit?

You walk us through one slow, repetitive process on a 30-minute video call. Within two working days you get a short written plan: what to automate first, what it will cost to build and run, and what it should save. It is yours to keep, and you can take it to anyone.

Who owns the automations and the accounts?

You do. Everything is built inside accounts in your name: your n8n, Make or Zapier workspace, your CRM, your API keys. If we stop working together you keep all of it, along with a handover document.

Is there a long contract?

No. The setup fee covers the build. The retainer runs month to month and you can cancel it with 30 days' notice.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

Each automation has rules for when to hand over to a person: an unusual request, a low-confidence answer, anything about money or health. During the pilot we review real conversations with you and tighten those rules before it runs on its own.

Do we need technical staff?

No. We build it, document it and run it. Your team needs one person who knows the current process well enough to tell us when the automation gets it wrong.

You are based in India. How does working together go?

By video call and a shared channel, with working hours that overlap the US, UK, UAE and Australia. You talk to the person who builds your automation, not an account manager.

What does it cost to run besides your fees?

The tools themselves: an automation platform, AI model usage, and phone or WhatsApp messaging if you use them. These are billed to your own accounts at cost, and the audit plan includes an estimate.
