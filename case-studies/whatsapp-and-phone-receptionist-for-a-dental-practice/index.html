A receptionist that answers every call and WhatsApp message

The front desk could not answer the phone and look after the patient in front of them at the same time. An AI receptionist now picks up every call and WhatsApp message, books routine visits, and passes anything urgent to a person.

The problem

The phone rang most during the morning rush, exactly when the front desk was checking patients in. Calls that went to voicemail were rarely returned the same day.

Patients also messaged the practice on WhatsApp, where replies depended on who had the practice phone that afternoon.

Reminders were sent by hand on quiet days and skipped on busy ones, so missed appointments rose in the busiest weeks.

What we built

The receptionist answers on the practice's existing number and WhatsApp line. It books, moves and cancels routine appointments in the practice diary, and answers the common questions about opening hours, parking and prices.

It never gives clinical advice. Anyone describing pain, swelling or bleeding is passed straight to a member of staff during hours, and given the emergency line out of hours.

We piloted on WhatsApp alone for three weeks before switching on the phone line, so the team could read every conversation and correct the wording.

The automations

Call and message answering
Every call and WhatsApp message is answered, day and night, in the practice's own tone.
Appointment booking
Check-ups and hygiene visits are booked, moved or cancelled directly in the diary.
Appointment reminders
A message two days before and again on the morning, with a one-tap option to rebook.
Patient recall
Patients due a check-up get a message offering three times. A reply books the visit.
Urgent handover
Pain or swelling is flagged to the front desk at once, with the patient's own words attached.

How it ran

The same four phases as every project.

  1. Audit

    Counted calls, voicemails and missed appointments for a month, and listed the 20 questions patients ask most.

    1 week

  2. Pilot

    WhatsApp only, routine bookings only. Front desk read every conversation at the end of each day.

    3 weeks

  3. Build

    Phone line, reminders, recall messages and the urgent handover, tested with staff acting as patients.

    5 weeks

  4. Retainer

    Conversation review, price and hours updates, and a monthly count of calls, bookings and handovers.

    Monthly
