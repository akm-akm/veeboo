The problem

Most enquiries arrived from two property portals and the brokerage's own website, and most of them arrived after 6pm. Whoever was on rota copied each one into the CRM by hand and called back when they could.

On a weekday that meant a reply within the hour. On a Saturday it could mean Monday. By then the buyer had usually spoken to another agent.

The owner did not want more software for agents to log in to. She wanted every lead answered quickly, and her agents talking only to people who were ready to view.

What we built

We started with one portal and one agent for three weeks. Once the replies read the way the team would write them, we added the second portal, the website form and the rest of the agents.

The assistant only does the first five minutes. It replies, asks about budget, area and timing, and offers viewing times from the agent's real calendar. A person takes over from there, with a short summary waiting in the CRM.

The automations

Instant lead reply
A text goes out within a minute of the enquiry, naming the property the buyer asked about.
Qualifying questions
Budget, preferred area and move date, asked one at a time in plain language.
Viewing booking
Free slots are read from the agent's calendar, and the booking is confirmed to both sides.
Handover summary
The CRM record is created with the answers and a three-line summary, so the agent never starts cold.
Quiet-lead nudge
If a buyer goes silent, one polite follow-up is sent the next morning, then the lead is passed to a person.

How it ran

The same four phases as every project.

  1. Audit

    Followed 40 recent leads from enquiry to first call, and timed every gap along the way.

    1 week

  2. Pilot

    One portal, one agent. Every reply was reviewed daily and the wording adjusted with the team.

    3 weeks

  3. Build

    Second portal, website form, all 12 calendars, the CRM handover and the morning nudge.

    4 weeks

  4. Retainer

    Reply wording reviewed each month, new listings and agents added, and a one-page report for the owner.
