AI agents and chatbots

An assistant on your website, WhatsApp or inbox that answers, qualifies and books, then hands over to a person when it should.

Who it is for

Businesses that answer the same questions all day, on the website, in the inbox or on WhatsApp, and lose the enquiries that arrive after hours.

Automations it includes

  • Website chat that qualifiesIt asks the three or four questions you would ask, then books a call or passes the enquiry to you.
  • Inbox triage and draft repliesIncoming email is sorted and a reply is drafted in your voice. A person approves it before it goes.
  • Answers from your own documentsIt answers from your price list, policies and FAQs, and says so when it does not know.

What you get

Questions owners ask before they start

Do I need to change the tools I already use?

Usually not. We build around the CRM, calendar, inbox and phone system you have. If one of them cannot be connected, you hear that in the audit, before any money changes hands.

Who owns the automations once they are built?

You do. Everything is built in accounts registered to your business, and we are invited in as a user. If we part ways, you remove our access and everything keeps running.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong?

Every build has written rules for what it may do alone and what needs a person. A failed step raises an alert and the item is kept, so nothing is dropped without anyone knowing. Fixes are part of the retainer.

Where does my customer data go?

It stays in your own accounts and the tools you choose. The AI model accounts are opened in your name, so their data terms are between you and the provider, and we go through those terms with you before anything goes live.

Can I start with one service and add more later?

Yes, and that is the better way to do it. Start with one narrow workflow in a pilot, look at the numbers after a month, then decide what to add.
