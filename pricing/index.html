What every plan includes

The same terms apply whether you buy one automation or ten.

  • You own the accounts. Everything is built in tools registered to your business, and we are invited in as a user.
  • No lock-in. If you leave, you remove our access and everything keeps running as it was.
  • The retainer is month to month. Stop it with 30 days' notice.
  • The person you speak to on the audit call is the person who builds it.
  • A written map and a video walkthrough of everything that is built.

What the retainer actually covers

Monitoring
Every run is watched. A failed step raises an alert, and the failed item is kept and retried.
Fixes
When a tool changes its settings or a connection breaks, it is repaired at no extra charge.
Small changes
New wording, an extra field, a changed rule. Each plan has a set number of hours a month for these.
Model and API costs
We watch what the AI models cost you each month, flag anything unusual and switch to a cheaper model where the results hold up.

What is not included

These are billed by the provider to your own accounts, at their prices, with no markup from us. Your written plan lists each one and its expected monthly cost before you commit.

  • Subscriptions to third-party tools, such as n8n, Make, Zapier or your CRM
  • AI model usage on the OpenAI API or Claude API
  • Voice minutes, phone numbers and WhatsApp Business message fees
  • New automations outside the agreed scope, which are quoted separately

Pricing questions

Why is there a setup fee and a retainer?

They pay for different work. The setup fee covers mapping the process, building, testing and handing over. The retainer covers keeping it running: tools change, connections break and your business changes.

Can I stop the retainer?

Yes, with 30 days' notice. The automations stay in your accounts and keep running. What stops is our monitoring, fixes and changes.

What will the third-party tools cost me?

It depends on the tools and the volume, so there is no honest single figure. Your written plan lists every tool the build needs and its expected monthly cost, and you see that before you commit.

What if the pilot does not work?

The pilot is kept narrow so that you find out in weeks, not months. If it misses the measure we agreed, you can stop there. You keep what was built, and no retainer starts.

Do you charge for the audit?

No. It is a 30-minute call and a written plan, and the plan is yours to keep whether or not you hire us.

Will the price change once work has started?

Not unless the scope does. The plan has a fixed quote. If you ask for something outside it, you get a separate price before any work is done.
